These past few days and weeks lahat tayo hindi nagkaroon nang proper communication. Some of us ay mataas ang pride, may takot mag reach out, and such. Since I have the freedom na to speak my mind, kahit dito na lang, magiging prangka na ako sa lahat ng bagay. First thing first, wala na po 'yung tampo about shooting. It's kind of immature naman kung dahil lang doon magpapakasira ang lahat. We understand, and respect you guys. Hindi po namimilit si cig na sumama ang lahat. Kalaunan naman naging okay na 'yon, nagkaroon pa nga po tayo ng confrontation during our first shoot with, tata queen.

There are things na we can't say directly the other reason why, but now gusto ko na siyang ilabas, not because para may ma-guilt, but para mailabas na 'yung bigat sa kalooban. I know naman po na lab na lab mo kaming lahat, mami, pero we have a biriii smooool heart. Siguro for me nanibago lang po ako sa naging treatment mo sa akin or sa amin when someone new entered our family. As for cig again, I know po na nagtampo na rin siya noon when you introduce us, kami ni dy, sa family. Suddenly nag click agad tayong tatlo na naging reason kung bakit naging active 'yung gc that I was once in that place. ʘ ‿ ʘ Napansin ko po kasi na mabilis ma divert 'yung attention niyo kapag may bago, not in a bad way naman po siya para sa akin. A little good thing kasi hindi naman dapat na sa amin lang umiikot ang mundo. It's an RP family only, an online world to be exact. Medyo masakit lang sa part na sobrang dali ko pala talaga bitawan, paano pa kaya kila cay, andcig na almost 2 years na sa sakamoto fam. I always think of them na sana piliin din nilang maayos kaysa umalis nang walang pasabi. Kahit h'wag na ako. Sinusubukan kong ipaalala sa kanila na sana maisip niyo rin ang lahat ng pinagsamahan, kulitan, at tawanan. This may sound ridiculous, but there were times we felt left behind. Wala naman po kaming galit kahit kanino. Siguro nanibago lang kami nang unti-unting nagbago ang dynamics ng family. Kaya siguro nagtampo kami and at the same time nagselos. As I said earlier kayo po 'yung lagi naming kasama sa lahat noon pa lang, pero iniwan niyo kami. At doon na rin nag start 'yung feeling of being left out. Yes, there were times na sinubukan kong mag reached out para maayos na ang lahat, but it seems that no one's looking for us anymore, and the bond you shared became stronger when we disappeared. Maybe that wasn't anyone's intention, but that's honestly how I felt. Kilala niyo na po akong kalog na tao, pero when too much emotions hit, Instead of speaking up, I chose silence na ugali ko talaga ever since. -_- Looking back, maybe that wasn't the best decision. I was trying to protect myself because I genuinely didn't know how to explain what I was feeling. Pero sana po binigyan niyo kahit ako ng chance na kusang mag salita ulit. Hindi ko lang po talaga kaya pa, lalo na at kapag anytime maglapag ako ng message sa gc masira ang happy moments niyong lahat. I don't want that naman. That's why binabawasan ko na ang dramas sa buhay ko kasi We all have our own silent battles hindi natin pinapaalam iba. And these past few days, hindi po talaga ako okay. Kaya kaming tatlo ang palaging magkakasama, and by that na le-lessen 'yung mga iniisip ko, and such. Nagpaka-busy ako, kami, para lang hindi na dumagdag pa sa mga problema sa buhay. Magsasabi naman ako anytime, it takes time po eh. There were so many times I wantedtosay, "Can we all just talk? Sobrang liit lang na bagay oh" I wanted us to fix things before they becamebigger. But every time, everyone backed out. Maybe because of pride. Maybe because of fear. Maybe because we thought we would just make things worse, kapag may hindi magandang nasabi ang bawat isa.
